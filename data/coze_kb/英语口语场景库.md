# 英语口语场景库

## 场景：餐厅点餐
情境：餐厅点餐
### 对话示范
- **coach**：Welcome to the restaurant! Let’s practice ordering food. How would you like to start?
- **user**：I’d like a salad and a steak.
- **coach**：Great choice! Let me confirm your order. Do you want any drinks with it?
- **user**：Yes, please add a glass of wine.
- **coach**：Your order is ready. Enjoy your meal!
- **user**：Thank you for the meal!
- **coach**：You’re welcome! Have a nice day!
### 关键句型
- Can I have a...?
- I’d like... please
- I’m sorry, I can’t have...
- Could you please...?
- Your order is ready.
### 常见错误
- 将‘order’拼写为‘ordar’
- 混淆‘please’和‘your’
- 错误使用‘what’s’代替‘what’s’

## 场景：机场问路
情境：机场问路
### 对话示范
- **coach**：Hello, can you tell me the way to the terminal?
- **user**：Sure! I need to go to the terminal 1.
- **coach**：Is there a bus to the terminal?
- **user**：Yes, there is a bus.
- **coach**：Which terminal are you going to?
- **user**：Terminal 2.
- **coach**：Do you need to take the 3号线?
- **user**：Yes, I need to take the 3号线.
### 关键句型
- Where do you need to go?
- Is there a bus to the terminal?
- Which terminal are you going to?
- Do you need to take the 3号线?
### 常见错误
- 拼写错误：将'terminal'拼成'termi-nal'
- 动词时态错误：用过去式'went'代替现在时'go'
- 介词错误：将'to'误用为'at'
- 不完整句子：缺少'yes'或'no'结尾

## 场景：职场沟通
情境：职场沟通
### 对话示范
- **coach**：Hello, how are you? I need to ask for your help with this project.
- **user**：Hello, I'm fine. Can you help me with this project?
- **coach**：Sure, I can help. Let me know what you need.
- **user**：I need to inform you about the deadline for the report.
- **coach**：Understood. Let me check the details for you.
- **user**：Thank you for your help. I really appreciate it.
### 关键句型
- Hello, how are you?
- Can you help me with this?
- I need to inform you about...
- Let me know what you need.
- Thank you for your help.
### 常见错误
- 用口语代替正式语（如'I'm fine'代替'How are you?'）
- 忽略对方感受（如不问对方需求直接说'Can you help me?'）
- 结构混乱（如对话中缺乏逻辑衔接）
- 未使用礼貌用语（如'you'代替'your'）
- 未明确请求内容（如'Can you help me'未说明具体需求）

## 场景：面试模拟
情境：面试模拟
### 对话示范
- **coach**：Hello, how are you today? How did you hear about this position?
- **user**：I heard about it through a friend. How should I prepare?
- **coach**：You can start with a brief introduction. What is your name and current job?
- **user**：My name is Alice, and I'm a student. What should I mention first?
- **coach**：Good point. Next, you can talk about your interests and why you're interested in this role. What's your biggest strength?
- **user**：I think teamwork is my strength. Can I share more?
- **coach**：Great! Now, what are your weaknesses? How would you improve them?
- **user**：I'm not good at public speaking. I'm working on it.
### 关键句型
- I am interested in this position because of the growth opportunities.
- What are your strengths and weaknesses?
- Can you tell me about your experience?
- Why do you want this job?
- What are your goals for the future?
- How do you handle challenges?
### 常见错误
- 将'Can you tell me about yourself?'误用于'Can you tell me about your job?'
- 混淆'Why do you want this position?'和'Why do you want to work here?'
- 忽略面试流程中的'What are your strengths?'和'What are your weaknesses?'问题
- 使用不完整的回答，如'Yes, I think...'而非'Yes, I think I can...'

## 场景：商场购物
情境：商场购物
### 对话示范
- **coach**：Hello! Let's practice shopping in the mall. How are you today?
- **user**：Hi! I'm feeling great. I want to buy a new shirt.
- **coach**：Great! Let's look for a shirt. What size do you prefer?
- **user**：I think size M is best. It fits well.
- **coach**：Perfect! Now let's check the price. Is it 50 yuan?
- **user**：Yes, it's 50 yuan. I'll buy it.
### 关键句型
- Look for a shirt that fits well
- Ask about the price and size
- Buy something you like
- Check the price before buying
- Thank you for the good service
### 常见错误
- 混淆 'look for' 和 'look at'（如 'I look for a shirt' vs 'I look at a shirt'）
- 误用 'buy' 和 'buying'（如 'I buy it' vs 'I am buying it'）
- 混淆 'size' 和 'sizeable'（如 'I need a size 10' vs 'It's sizeable'）
- 忘记使用 'please' 和 'thank you'（如 'I want to buy' vs 'Please help me'）

## 场景：校园交流
情境：校园交流
### 对话示范
- **coach**：Good question! Let me show you how to ask for directions.
- **user**：Excuse me, may I ask for directions?
- **coach**：Perfect! You can say 'Excuse me, may I ask for directions?'
- **user**：Could you lend me the book?
- **coach**：Sure, I can lend it to you.
- **user**：Thank you!
- **coach**：You're welcome! No problem.
### 关键句型
- Excuse me, may I ask for directions?
- Could you lend me...?
- Thank you!
- You're welcome!
- No problem.
### 常见错误
- 忘记使用'Excuse me'或'May I'开头
- 错误使用'lend'或'give'时遗漏介词
- 混淆'You're welcome'与'Thank you'
- 不使用'Please'或'Can you'等礼貌表达

## 场景：酒店入住
情境：酒店入住
### 对话示范
- **coach**：Hello! Welcome to the hotel. Can you tell me the room rate?
- **user**：The room rate is $100 per night.
- **coach**：That's the standard rate. Do you need a reservation?
- **user**：Yes, I can check in at 10 AM.
- **coach**：Great! Your room will be ready at 10 AM. Do you need help with anything else?
- **user**：I'll take a shower first. Can I check out at 10 AM?
- **coach**：Of course. Your check-out time is 10 AM.
### 关键句型
- How much is the room fee?
- Can I check in at 10 AM?
- Do you have a room for tonight?
- I'll take a shower first.
- Your check-out time is 10 AM.
### 常见错误
- 混淆'check in'和'check out'的时态（如'checked in'应为'checked in'）
- 误用'room fee'和'room rate'（如'room fee is $100'应为'room rate is $100'）
- 忘记使用正确的介词（如'to check in'而非'to check in'）
